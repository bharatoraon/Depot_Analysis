Routing & Classification Rules

  • Nodes: 4,145 MTC Bus Stops, inferred and mapped Terminals, Metro, and Suburban stations within CMA.
  • Walking transfers: Automatically generated walking links between any two stops within 200 meters.
  • RAPTOR Routing: Explores the transit graph up to 3 rounds (2 transfers/3 buses).
  • Representative selection: Coordinates projected to meters (EPSG:32644). The geographically closest reachable terminal/hub is selected.
  • Category Assignment: Connectivity category (Direct, 2 routes, 3 routes, etc.) is determined by the minimum transfers required to reach that closest hub.
  • Underserved Focus: Isolates stops requiring 2+ transfers (3+ routes/buses) or disconnected stops to identify transit deserts.

Public Transport Accessibility Level (PTAL)

PTAL measures walking access and transit service density from a stop's perspective using a standardized door-to-door transit accessibility index.

  • Walking Access Time: Calculates walk times from a stop to all neighboring nodes within 640m (buses/terminals) and 960m (metro/suburban rail), assuming a walk speed of 80 meters/minute (4.8 km/h).
  • Scheduled Wait Time (SWT): Calculated using empirical schedules from MTC & CMRL GTFS datasets for the selected period (Morning Peak: 08:00-10:00, Midday Off-Peak: 12:00-14:00, Evening Peak: 17:00-19:00) where available, plus mode reliability margins.
    • Empirical Headways: Direct lookup of exact scheduled peak headways per route and stop. Mapped CMRL Metro parent stations spatially (within 250m) to resolve name mismatches (e.g., CMBT).
    • Suburban Rail: Applied a standard 20.0-minute peak headway default (as timetables are not public).
    • Heuristic Fallback: If GTFS schedule data is missing, we fall back to a route-count headway proxy (>10 routes: 5m, 5-10 routes: 10m, 2-4 routes: 15m, 1 route: 30m).
    • Reliability Margins: Bus = 2.0 min, Metro = 0.75 min, Suburban = 1.50 min.
    • Wait Time Formula: SWT = (0.5 * Headway) + ReliabilityMargin
  • Accessibility Index (AI): The dominant route (minimum access time $AT_{dom}$) is weighted fully, while all other accessible routes are weighted at 50%:
    AI = (30 / AT_dom) + 0.5 * Σ (30 / AT_non_dom)
  • PTAL Grades: AI is mapped to grades 0 (Very Poor) to 6b (Excellent) based on London standards.

Network Health Index (NHI)

NHI evaluates the quality, reliability, and redundancy of transit services from a stop's perspective on a scale of 0 to 100.

  • Directness Score (30%): Compares actual transit route distance to Euclidean straight-line distance to the closest hub. Circuity is defined as RouteDistance / EuclideanDistance.
    • Empirical Routing Distance: Calculated by accumulating stop-to-stop segment distances along the GTFS stop sequence for buses, bypassing shape projection errors. Falls back to shape-based projection for non-GTFS routes.
    • Directness Score Formula: S_directness = max(0, 100 * (2.0 - Circuity))
  • Transfer Friction Score (30%): Assesses ease of connection to key hubs:
    • Direct connection: 100 points
    • 1 transfer (2 routes): 70 points
    • 2 transfers (3 routes): 30 points
    • 3+ transfers or disconnected: 0 points
  • Multi-Modal Integration (20%): Awards 100 points if the stop is within 200m walking distance of a Metro or Suburban Rail station (acting as a multimodal transfer point), otherwise 0 points.
  • Network Resilience (20%): Measures protection against single-line failures through route redundancy:
    S_resilience = 100 * (1 - e^(-0.3 * (RoutesCount - 1)))

GPS-Empirical Operations & Performance Gaps

We integrated raw GPS telemetry (May 20, 2026) to evaluate real-world transit accessibility, operational reliability, and city-wide performance gaps across three distinct time periods: Morning Peak (08:00-10:00 AM), Midday Off-Peak (12:00-02:00 PM), and Evening Peak (05:00-07:00 PM IST).

  • GPS Data Ingestion: Ingested 5.5 GB of raw bus GPS data. Extracted and filtered pings matching the selected period's UTC window. Mapped pings to stops within 100m in O(1) lookup time using a 0.002-degree spatial grid index.
  • Empirical Headways: Identified discrete bus visits by grouping pings by vehicle ID and time, sorted arrivals chronologically, and calculated observed headway intervals per stop and route.
  • GPS-Empirical PTAL: Substituted GTFS scheduled headways with observed headways to calculate wait time (SWT = 0.5 * Headway_GPS + 2.0 mins), mapping real-world accessibility grades.
  • GPS-Empirical NHI: Replaced the theoretical Network Resilience score with two operational sub-scores (each weighted at 10%):
    • Headway Reliability (S_reliability): Penalizes service irregularity based on the Coefficient of Variation (CV = std_dev / mean) of stop headways.
    • Travel Speed (S_speed): Derived dynamically from consecutive ping coordinate displacements and time gaps to measure traffic congestion levels.
  • Performance Variance Mapping: Compares scheduled vs. empirical performance on a diverging Red-Gray-Green scale, highlighting service gains (Green) and bottleneck hotspots (Red) in a dedicated comparison view for each selected period.
